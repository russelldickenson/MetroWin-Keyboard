The Windows Phone keyboard, on Android.

• Flat Metro keys with big light type, in five looks (Classic, Modern Glass, Modern Droid, iPhone and Modern Slate), with one colour of your own, or a photo behind the keys
• Swipe typing, hold a key for accents and symbols, double space for a full stop, slide on the space bar to move the cursor
• Correction that you can undo with Backspace, next-word and phrase prediction, offensive words blocked, everything learned on your phone only
• 17 languages included; add any language or dictionary yourself
• Clipboard history with pictures, a quick button on the bar, emoji and classic emoticons, voice typing
• Adjustable key height, width and spacing; sound (Windows Phone style) and vibration with adjustable strength
• A guided set-up and a tutorial, in the app and inside the keyboard

No ads, no analytics, no trackers. Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). Not affiliated with Microsoft.
